Orders questions

Last updated October 1, 2026

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, while it is Placed, usually the first 30 minutes. After that, staff can help but changes are not guaranteed. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Only while the order is Placed. After that, contact staff.

How many phones can I buy in one order?

Up to 2 of the same phone model per order.

How many tablets or accessories can I order?

Up to 5 per category per order.

What does Processing mean?

We are preparing your order; it can no longer be changed online.

What does Delayed mean?

Your order is late and we are working with the carrier. We will update you.

What does Ready for pickup mean?

Your order is waiting at the store for 7 days.

How do I check my order status?

Ask the assistant with your order number. Guests verify with a one-time code.

Can I order a product that is out of stock?

Out-of-stock items cannot be ordered. Ask the assistant to check another store for pickup.

Can I pre-order the MacBook Pro 14-inch (M6)?

Yes. Pre-orders opened on October 28, 2026 and orders ship from November 6, 2026.
