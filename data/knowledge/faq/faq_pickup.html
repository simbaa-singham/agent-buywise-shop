Pickup questions

Last updated October 1, 2026

Is in-store pickup free?

Yes, pickup is always free.

How soon can I pick up my order?

Usually within 2 hours if the item is in stock at your store, or in 2-4 days if it is transferred from another store.

How long will the store hold my order?

7 days from the day it is ready. After that it is cancelled and you are not charged.

Can someone else pick up my order?

Yes, one alternate person named at checkout, with a valid photo ID.

Can I change the pickup person after ordering?

Yes, ask the assistant to connect you with staff.

What do I need to bring?

Your order number and a valid photo ID.

Until what time can I pick up?

Until 30 minutes before the store closes.

What are the store hours?

Monday to Saturday 10 am to 9 pm, Sunday 11 am to 6 pm. Holiday hours differ; see the holiday notice.

Are stores open on Thanksgiving?

No. All stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, November 26, 2026.

What are the Black Friday hours?

8 am to 10 pm on Friday, November 27, 2026.
