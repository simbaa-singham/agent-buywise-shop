Shipping questions

Last updated October 1, 2026

Do you ship outside the United States?

No. We ship to US addresses only.

How long does Standard delivery take?

3-5 business days in the 48 contiguous states.

Is shipping free?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 (policy version 1, in force until November 14, 2026). From November 15, 2026 the threshold is $75. Otherwise Standard costs $5.99.

How much is Express delivery?

$14.99, delivered in 1-2 business days.

Can I get same-day delivery?

Yes, for $9.99, if you order by 12 pm local time and your address is within 20 miles of a BuyWise store.

Do you deliver to Alaska and Hawaii?

Yes. Standard takes 5-8 business days and Express 2-4 business days. Same-day is not available.

Can I ship to a PO box?

Yes for orders up to $1,000 using Standard delivery. Orders over $1,000 need a street address.

Will I need to sign for my delivery?

Yes, if your order is over $500.

What happens if I miss my delivery?

The carrier tries 3 times, then holds the package for 5 days. If it is not collected, it is returned and the order is cancelled.

When do I get a tracking number?

As soon as your order ships, by email and in your order details.

Is the free shipping threshold changing?

Yes. From November 15, 2026, Standard shipping is free on orders over $75 instead of $50.
