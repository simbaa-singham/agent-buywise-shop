Warranty questions

Last updated October 1, 2026

What warranty do products have?

The manufacturer's 1-year limited warranty from the delivery or pickup date.

Is a cracked screen covered?

No. Accidental damage such as drops and cracked screens is not covered.

Is water damage covered?

No, liquid damage is not covered.

Is a faulty battery covered?

Yes, if it fails under normal use within the warranty period.

How do I make a warranty claim?

Ask the assistant to connect you with staff, or visit a store, with your order number and serial number.

Can the assistant process my warranty claim?

No. It explains coverage and connects you with staff to start the claim.

Are scratches covered?

No, cosmetic wear such as scratches and dents is not covered.
