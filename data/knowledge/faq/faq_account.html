Account questions

Last updated October 1, 2026

Do I need an account to buy?

No, guest checkout is available.

How do guests check order status?

With a one-time code sent to the email or phone on the order.

How long is a one-time code valid?

10 minutes, with 3 attempts.

I entered the wrong code three times. What now?

Wait 15 minutes, then request a new code.

Will staff ask me for my code?

No. BuyWise staff never ask you to read out a one-time code.

How long do you keep chat transcripts?

90 days, then they are deleted or anonymized.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Ask staff; your profile, conversations and tickets are deleted and orders are kept without your identity.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes, ask our staff for a data export.

Can I return a product?

The assistant does not handle returns. Ask to be connected with staff or visit a store.

Do you offer trade-ins?

No, there is no trade-in program.

When can I talk to a person?

Staff are available in chat 8 am to 10 pm Eastern Time daily; outside those hours a ticket is created and staff reply the next day.
