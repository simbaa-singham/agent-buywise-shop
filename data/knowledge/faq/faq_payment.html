Payment questions

Last updated October 1, 2026

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit cards, debit cards and digital wallets, through a secure payment link.

My payment link expired. What do I do?

Ask for a new link. Links expire after 30 minutes and your cart is kept.

When will I be charged?

Your payment is authorized at order time and charged when the order ships or is picked up.

How is sales tax calculated?

From your delivery address, or from the store's location for pickup.

Do you offer financing?

No, financing and installment plans are not offered.

Can I use a gift card?

No, gift cards are not accepted.

Can I split payment between two cards?

No, each order uses one payment method.

Does the assistant see my card number?

No. Payment happens on a secure payment page; BuyWise never stores full card numbers.

Do you price match?

No, we do not offer price matching.

If the price drops after I order, do I get the lower price?

No. You pay the price shown when you placed the order.
