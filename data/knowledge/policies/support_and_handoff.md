# Support and Talking to a Person

Effective date: January 1, 2026.

BuyWise staff are available in chat from 8 am to 10 pm Eastern Time, every day. Ask the assistant for a person at any time. Outside those hours, the assistant creates a support ticket and our staff reply the next day.

The assistant also offers to connect you with staff when it cannot answer a question, when a request is outside what it can do (for example returns or warranty claims), or when you seem frustrated.
