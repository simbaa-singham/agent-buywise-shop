# Returns, Exchanges, Refunds and Trade-ins

Effective date: January 1, 2026.

The BuyWise shopping assistant does not handle returns, exchanges, refunds or trade-ins. If you want to return or exchange a product, ask the assistant to connect you with our staff, or visit any BuyWise store. Staff will explain the options for your order.

BuyWise does not currently offer a trade-in program.
