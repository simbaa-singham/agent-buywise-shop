Set up an eSIM

  1. Some models, such as iPhone Air, support eSIM only and have no physical SIM tray.
  2. During setup, choose to transfer your number from your old phone or scan your carrier's QR code.
  3. If your carrier is not listed, contact the carrier for an eSIM activation code.
  4. Restart the phone after activation and make a test call.
