# Track your order

1. Ask the BuyWise assistant 'Where is my order?' and give your order number, for example BW-100002.
2. Signed-in customers see all their orders; guests verify with a one-time code.
3. When your order ships you receive a tracking number by email.
4. If your order shows Delayed, staff are already working with the carrier.
