# Get the fastest wired charging

1. Use a USB-C cable and a USB-C power adapter.
2. iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max support up to 60 W wired charging with a suitable adapter.
3. Other phones charge fast with a 30-40 W adapter; a 20 W adapter still works, just slower.
4. Charging slows down above 80% to protect the battery.
