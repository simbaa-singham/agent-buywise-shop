Pair your watch with a phone

  1. Turn on the watch by holding the side button until the logo appears.
  2. Hold the watch near your phone and wait for the pairing screen.
  3. Point the phone camera at the watch face to pair, then follow the prompts.
  4. Choose your band size settings and wrist preference, then let the watch sync.
