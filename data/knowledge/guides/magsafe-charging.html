Charge with MagSafe

  1. Connect the MagSafe Charger to a USB-C power adapter of 30 W or more for the fastest wireless charging.
  2. Place the phone on the charger; the magnets align it automatically.
  3. MagSafe cases can stay on. Remove other thick cases, wallets or metal objects.
  4. Qi2 devices also charge on a MagSafe Charger, usually at lower power.
