Set up your new phone and move your data

  1. Charge your new phone and keep your old phone nearby with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on.
  2. Turn on the new phone and follow the on-screen setup until you see the option to transfer data.
  3. Hold the old phone near the new one and follow the prompts to start a direct transfer.
  4. Keep both phones close together and connected to power until the transfer finishes.
  5. Sign in to your accounts and check that your apps, photos and messages arrived.
