Set up a new laptop

  1. Connect the laptop to power and open the lid; it turns on automatically.
  2. Follow the setup assistant to join Wi-Fi and sign in.
  3. Use the migration assistant to move files from an older computer if needed.
  4. MacBook Pro 14-inch (M6) pre-orders ship from November 6, 2026.
