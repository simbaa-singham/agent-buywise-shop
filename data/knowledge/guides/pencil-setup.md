# Pair Pencil Pro or Pencil (USB-C) with a tablet

1. Pencil Pro pairs by attaching it magnetically to the side of a compatible tablet.
2. Pencil Pro works with iPad Pro (M5), iPad Air (M4) and iPad mini (A17 Pro), but not with iPad (A16).
3. Pencil (USB-C) pairs by connecting it to the tablet with a USB-C cable and works with every tablet we sell.
