# Check if your warranty covers a problem

1. Every product has a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery or pickup date.
2. Defects are covered; drops, cracked screens, liquid damage and cosmetic wear are not.
3. To start a claim, ask the assistant to connect you with staff and have your order number ready.
