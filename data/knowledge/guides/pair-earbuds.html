Pair your AirPods with a phone

  1. Open the charging case next to your unlocked phone.
  2. Tap Connect on the setup card that appears on the phone.
  3. For AirPods Pro 3 and AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation, choose a listening mode after pairing.
  4. If no card appears, press and hold the button on the case until the light flashes white, then try again.
