# Compatibility notes

## Cases are model-specific

A case fits exactly one phone model. A case for iPhone 18 Pro does not fit iPhone 18 Pro Max or iPhone 17, even though they look similar.

## MagSafe and Qi2

Phones with MagSafe or Qi2 charge on the MagSafe Charger. iPhone Duo is not listed as MagSafe compatible in our catalog; use USB-C.

## Watch bands

Bands come in two size groups. The smaller band fits 40 mm and 42 mm cases. The larger band fits 44 mm, 46 mm and 49 mm cases.

## Pencils

Pencil Pro does not work with iPad (A16). Pencil (USB-C) works with every tablet we sell.

## Power adapters

Any USB-C power adapter charges any USB-C device. Laptops and 60 W phones charge slowly on a 20 W adapter.

## Laptops and MagSafe

MagSafe 3 on laptops is a different connector from MagSafe phone charging; the phone MagSafe Charger does not charge laptops.
